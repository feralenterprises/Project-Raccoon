R3 - play and observe (about 20 minutes). The test plugin is designed only to watch; it does not act in the game.
Only normal worlds. Never hop to PvP, Deadman, Seasonal, Beta or other special worlds for this test.
You need a second account for step 10: one you own, or one whose owner has agreed to this test.

 1. On the login screen, click the wrench (Configuration) in the right-hand sidebar, search for
    "Project Raccoon" and switch it OFF. Do not touch "Raccoon Playtest Witness".
 2. Sign in. Jagex account: Jagex account icon in the sidebar, "Add Jagex Account", sign in,
    pick your character, "Play Now". Older account: type your details on the login screen.
 3. Wait 20 seconds in the game, then switch "Project Raccoon" back ON. Wait 30 seconds.
 4. Open your bank (enter your PIN if asked) and leave it open for at least 1 minute.
 5. With the bank still open: click the bank's worn-equipment button to change the view, wait
    5 seconds, switch back; open the bank's settings/menu for 5 seconds and close it again.
    (This may or may not produce the "failed read" the test is looking for; that is fine.)
    Leave the bank open for another 30 seconds.
 6. Close the bank. Wait 10 seconds. Open it again for 30 seconds, then close it.
 7. Hop once to another normal world of the same kind. Wait 30 seconds after you arrive.
 8. Kill Brutus twice in a row without hopping or logging out in between
    (genuine kills; Demonic Brutus does not count).
 9. Switch "Project Raccoon" OFF, wait 10 seconds, switch it ON again, wait 30 seconds.
10. Log out. Sign in with the second account, wait 30 seconds, open and close its bank, log out.
11. Sign in with your first account again and keep playing normally until at least 10 minutes
    have passed since step 3.
12. Close the client window normally (the X). The results appear here shortly after.
Anything not observed is reported as BLOCKED. That is not your fault; the reviewer will say
whether a step should be repeated.
