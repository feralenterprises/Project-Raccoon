R3 - play and observe (about 15 minutes). The test plugin only watches; it never acts in the game.
Only normal worlds. Never hop to PvP, Deadman, Seasonal, Beta or other special worlds for this test.

 1. Sign in. Jagex account: click the Jagex account icon in the client's right-hand sidebar,
    choose "Add Jagex Account", sign in in the window that opens, pick your character, then
    press "Play Now". Older (non-Jagex) account: type your details on the login screen.
 2. Stand still for 30 seconds after you arrive in the game.
 3. Open your bank (enter your bank PIN if you have one). Keep it open for at least 1 minute.
 4. Close the bank. Wait 10 seconds. Open it again for 10 seconds, then close it.
 5. Hop once to another normal world of the same kind (members to members, free to free).
    Wait 30 seconds after you arrive.
 6. Kill Brutus at least twice (genuine kills; Demonic Brutus does not count).
    Optional: hop once more after the second kill, then kill Brutus one more time.
 7. Log out to the login screen, log back in, and wait 30 seconds.
 8. Keep playing normally until at least 10 minutes have passed since step 2.
 9. Close the client window. The results appear here a few seconds later.
